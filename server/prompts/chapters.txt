Rewrite the source chapter into a sharper, more useful technical chapter note.

Outcome:
- Produce a polished standalone write-up in the original language of the source chapter.
- Preserve the real technical meaning, architecture, constraints, tradeoffs, and correctness concerns.
- Improve the material for actual understanding instead of preserving interview-style verbosity.
- Keep the result concise, high-signal, and logically structured.

Keep And Strengthen:
- real problem framing
- important requirements and constraints
- scale assumptions that affect the design
- core design choices and why they fit
- tradeoffs between plausible approaches
- data model, APIs, write path, read path, and operational flow when they matter
- correctness concerns, failure modes, recovery behavior, and product-facing consequences
- the first scaling boundary where the simple solution stops working

Remove Or Compress:
- repetitive interviewer/candidate dialogue
- long introductions and filler wrap-up sections
- obvious statements that do not add design insight
- generic API boilerplate unless it changes the architecture
- broad textbook explanations unless they are directly relevant to this system
- generic system design advice that could apply to any problem
- low-value implementation trivia that does not affect a design decision

Writing Rules:
- Write in the original language of the source chapter. Do not translate to English unless the source is English.
- Prefer explanation over transcription.
- Compress obvious material.
- Expand subtle or important material enough to make the design easier to understand.
- Do not invent requirements, numbers, APIs, benchmarks, incidents, or design decisions not supported by the source.
- Do not pad the text to match the original length.
- Preserve useful source terminology and proper nouns.

Structure:
- Use clear markdown section headings.
- Keep a logical flow such as problem, requirements, scale, classification, design, correctness, recovery, scaling limits, and final recommendation.
- Do not use markdown tables unless they are essential to preserve meaning.
- Avoid bullet-heavy output; use prose by default.

Output:
- Output only the rewritten chapter note.
- Do not add preambles, process notes, or commentary outside the write-up.
